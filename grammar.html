Quick Grammar Rules to Remember
🔚

Verb always goes at the end of the sentence

🏷️

Particles (は, を, に, の) label what each word does

❓

Add か at the end to ask a question

🙅

No articles like 'a' or 'the' in Japanese

🔢

No plural forms — いぬ means both 'dog' and 'dogs'

😊

です (desu) = polite 'is/am/are' — use it to sound friendly

In English we say: I eat sushi. In Japanese the verb comes LAST: I sushi eat! (わたしは すしを たべます)

Verb goes at the END

わたしは ねこが すきです

Watashi wa neko ga suki desu

"I like cats"

Breaking it down:

わたしはI (topic)
ねこがcats (subject)
すきですlike (verb at end!)
No spaces needed between words

これはほんです

Kore wa hon desu

"This is a book"

Breaking it down:

これはthis (topic)
ほんbook
ですis
